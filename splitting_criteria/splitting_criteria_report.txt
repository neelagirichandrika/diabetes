Investigating Decision Tree Splitting Criteria:
  1. Gini Impurity: G = 1 - sum(p_i^2)
  2. Entropy / Log-Loss: H = - sum(p_i * log2(p_i))

Experimental Results (max_depth=6):
           Accuracy  F1_Score  ROC_AUC  Train_Time_s
Criterion                                           
GINI          62.25    0.5549   0.6579          0.93
ENTROPY       62.15    0.5453   0.6580          1.05
LOG_LOSS      62.15    0.5453   0.6580          1.06

Findings:
- Gini Impurity and Entropy achieve nearly identical ROC-AUC on this dataset.
- Gini executes faster computationally because it avoids logarithmic operations.
- Gini impurity is retained as default for tree-based experiments.
